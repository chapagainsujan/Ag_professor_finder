# Funding databases

Goal: for each shortlisted professor, find out what money backs their work now, who the PI is, and when it ends. Money tells you what the lab will still be doing in two years and whether it can pay a student.

URLs and program names change. Treat every link here as a starting point and confirm on the live page. Funding portals often block automated fetching; if a page will not load, search for the project title instead and use the funder's news page or the university's news page.

## How to read a hit

Record: funder, program, project title, PI and co-PIs, start and end date, amount (if shown), status.
- **PI vs co-PI.** A co-PI on a big cluster may have little say over hiring. Say which.
- **End date.** Under 12 months left means the lab may not be recruiting for that project, but a renewal may be coming. Mention it, do not guess.
- **Eligibility is about the institution, not the student.** Most programs below fund the professor's university. They do not give grants to a student abroad. Do not tell the student they can apply for these. They matter because they show the professor can fund an assistantship.
- **No record is not proof of no money.** Industry contracts, start-up funds, and some state funds are not public.

## United States

| Source | What it holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| USDA NIFA data gateway and the NIFA Reporting Portal (CRIS) | Funded and ongoing research projects with PI, institution, dates, funding, project summaries | Best single source for US ag. Search by PI surname, institution, or keyword. https://www.nifa.usda.gov/data/data-gateway |
| NIFA AFRI | Main competitive federal ag program: plant health, breeding, production, pest management, systems, education | Applicant institutions must be US ones. Check each notice |
| NIFA SCRI (Specialty Crop Research Initiative) | Fruit, vegetable, nut, nursery, floriculture research plus extension | Funded project pages list teams |
| NIFA OREI (organic) | Organic production, breeding, pest and disease | |
| NIFA Crop Protection and Pest Management | Pest, pathogen and weed management research and extension | |
| USDA SARE | Regional sustainable-agriculture grants | Regional offices list funded projects by state |
| USDA AMS Specialty Crop Block Grant | State-level specialty crop projects | Applications go through state departments of agriculture |
| U.S. Wheat and Barley Scab Initiative (USWBSI) | Fusarium head blight research | PIs at US land-grant universities or USDA-ARS. Funded-project lists name teams and topics. https://scabusa.org |
| International Wheat Yield Partnership (IWYP) | International wheat-yield research | Check page for current calls and past awards |
| NSF Award Search | Basic science awards (plant genome, biology, engineering) | https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch and API https://resources.research.gov/common/webapi/awardapisearch-v1.htm |
| USAspending.gov, Grants.gov | All federal awards and open calls | Good fallback for "who got what" |
| State and commodity boards | Corn, soybean, cotton, wheat, potato, citrus, peanut, etc. | Each board posts funded projects. Search "<crop> checkoff research funded projects <state>" |
| USDA-ARS project pages | ARS scientists are not professors, but many hold adjunct posts and co-supervise | Useful for co-advisor leads |

Search patterns:
- `"<professor name>" NIFA project`
- `"<professor name>" "principal investigator" USDA`
- `<keyword> site:nifa.usda.gov` and `<keyword> site:portal.nifa.usda.gov`
- `<crop> <state> commodity checkoff funded research <year>`

## Canada

| Source | What it holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NSERC awards database | Discovery, Alliance, CRD and other grants by researcher | Open data copy on open.canada.ca ("NSERC's Awards Data"). Search by name, institution, keyword |
| Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC): AgriScience Program (Clusters and Projects) | Industry-led collaborative research | Cluster pages name participating universities and PIs |
| Canadian National Wheat Cluster (2023 to 2028) and Canadian Wheat Research Coalition | Wheat breeding, agronomy, disease | A map of who is working on wheat |
| AAFC research project database, AAFC grants and contributions database | Federal projects and awards | Records of awards, not application portals |
| Western Grains Research Foundation (WGRF) | Western Canada field-crop research | Project search by topic, researcher, institution |
| Saskatchewan Agriculture Development Fund (ADF) | Saskatchewan crop and livestock research | Project lists by year |
| Alberta RDAR (Results Driven Agriculture Research) | Alberta crop and livestock research | Funded project pages |
| Manitoba Sustainable CAP research and innovation | Manitoba | Check intake status |
| Ontario Agri-Food Research Initiative; Vineland Research and Innovation Centre | Ontario field crops, horticulture | Vineland is a research centre that co-supervises with universities |
| AgPal | Finder for federal, provincial and territorial programs | Use to discover programs by keyword |
| Canadian provincial commodity groups | Pulse Canada, Grain Farmers of Ontario, Canola Council, Saskatchewan Pulse Growers, etc. | Post funded projects |
| Mitacs | Industry-linked graduate internships | Another reason a professor may have partner money |

Canada has no single scab initiative like USWBSI. For FHB, search CWRC, WGRF, provincial programs and the AAFC project database for "Fusarium head blight", "FHB", "wheat disease resistance", "mycotoxin".

## Cross-cutting

- OpenAlex lists funders and award IDs for many papers. Acknowledgement sections in papers also name grant numbers. Read the last page of a recent paper: "Funding" or "Acknowledgements".
- University news pages announce big awards. Search `"<professor name>" awarded grant <year>`.
- Lab pages often have a "Funding" or "Sponsors" strip of logos. Open it.

## What to write in the dossier

```
Funding found:
- [Funder / program]: "[Project title]". Role: PI. [Start] to [End]. Source: [URL]
- ...
Funding not found in: [list of databases checked]
Student-relevant: [e.g. project ends 2027, extension plausible. Do not assume.]
```

## For a student applying from abroad

- Federal AFRI, USWBSI, Canadian cluster and ARC funds go to the host institution. A student gets paid through a research or teaching assistantship that the professor or department offers, or a scholarship the university offers.
- Scholarships to check separately: US university assistantships and fellowships, Canada's graduate scholarships and provincial programs, Australia's Research Training Program (RTP) and industry RDC scholarships (GRDC, Hort Innovation, MLA and others), and ACIAR fellowships for partner-country students. Deadlines are often months before the start date. Confirm every one on the official page.
